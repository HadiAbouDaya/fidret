# Fidret company charter

Established direction from Hadi's founding brief on 2026-10-24. Operating principles below are the initial implementation of that direction and can evolve through documented decisions.

## Name and meaning

**Fidret means Trust earned.** The name is inspired by the Latin *fīdere*, “to trust” or “to rely upon.” Its deeper idea is expertise that earns trust: clients can rely on our judgment, our work, and our commitment to deliver.

Use “inspired by” when explaining the origin. This is the founder's chosen meaning and naming story, rather than a literal Latin translation.

## Purpose

Build a company whose expertise earns clients' trust through sound judgment, useful work, and reliable delivery. Expertise is developed, demonstrated, and maintained through practice. Trust is earned through what people experience when working with us.

## Initial direction

Physical AI is the first practice to develop. Fidret is the company; practices organize areas of expertise beneath that company. A new technology, product, or market must serve the same purpose and be justified on its own evidence.

The initial model combines understanding a client's situation with hands-on technical work. The first target market, commercial offer, and technical approach will be selected through discovery and demonstrated capability.

## Operating principles

| Commitment | What it changes in our work |
| --- | --- |
| Exercise sound judgment | Understand the need, constraints, alternatives, and consequences before choosing an approach. Recommend stopping when the case does not justify continuing. |
| Make commitments clear | Agree scope, ownership, success criteria, dependencies, and delivery expectations. Surface changes early. |
| Ground claims in evidence | Distinguish an idea, an experiment, a pilot, and a deployed system. Preserve conditions and limitations with reported results. |
| Deliver work others can use | Include the implementation, validation, documentation, and handover needed for the agreed use. |
| Take responsibility | Admit errors, investigate failures, communicate their effect, and follow through on the correction. |
| Respect the people affected | Account for users, operators, client teams, and the consequences of systems acting in the physical world. |

## Decision questions

Before a substantial commitment, ask:

1. Whose problem are we solving, and what outcome matters to them?
2. What can we credibly deliver, and what still needs to be learned?
3. What evidence will establish success under the intended conditions?
4. What obligations will remain after delivery, and who will own them?

Record the answers in the task or engagement brief. A decision to narrow scope, seek a partner, or stop can earn trust when the reasoning is clear.

## Voice and claims

Speak plainly and precisely. Explain the client's need and the useful result before the technology. State ambitions as ambitions and results with their conditions. Use the tagline **Trust earned.** Do not publish invented case studies, customer counts, partnerships, delivery history, or claims of established physical AI expertise.

## Open choices

Market, offers, pricing, legal structure, brand assets, technical stack, and specific physical AI capabilities remain to be developed. Future work should record what changes, why it serves the purpose, and what evidence supports it.

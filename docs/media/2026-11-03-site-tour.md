## Production notes

The final edit uses Brave captures of Home, Physical AI, Approach, the signal walkthrough’s actual fresh/expired/recovered states, Lab, the workflow brief and the charter. A restrained closing frame uses Fidret’s own Manrope font and forest/ivory palette. The render is 1920 × 1080 at 30 fps; source browser captures are 1688 × 944, reframed with gentle zooms. It is an edited page-capture tour, not a continuous screen recording.

Narration uses Microsoft’s `en-GB-RyanNeural` synthetic voice through `edge-tts` 7.3.1. Word-boundary metadata supplies caption timing; punctuation follows the canonical script. FFmpeg 9.0.2, obtained through `imageio-ffmpeg` 0.8.1, composes H.264/AAC MP4 with fast-start metadata. Burned-in captions are accompanied by an optional embedded English subtitle track and an editable SRT sidecar. No new dependencies enter the website manifest or lockfile.

Production dependencies, captures, generated voice, timing data and intermediate renders remain in ignored local scratch storage. The MP4, SRT and transcript are delivered outside the repository. No external upload, social publication or live-site change is part of this task.

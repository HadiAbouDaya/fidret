# Software factory Lab: research and design basis

Checked 4 November 2026 for the [Lab task](../tasks/2026-11-04-ai-software-factory-lab.md). The canonical public narrative is [the architecture note](../../lab/pieces/ai-software-factory.md). This record preserves the research interpretation and design choices, not a second editable article or an adopted company operating policy.

## Evidence and synthesis

No reviewed source establishes a universally best AI software factory, unrestricted reliable autonomy, guaranteed productivity or complete safety. The proposed model combines established delivery/security practices with bounded agent execution. Human responsibility is an operating requirement with authority, knowledge, time and support, not an approval click or a legal conclusion about one engineer.

The software-factory framing builds on Hadi's previously published [lifecycle article](https://hadi.aboudaya.com/blog/ai-software-factory-beyond-coding-agents/) and [Michelin Principle follow-up](https://hadi.aboudaya.com/blog/michelin-principle-ai-software-factories/). Those are continuity references. The public Lab links primary evidence directly and develops a different, interactive teaching artifact.

## Experience choices

One fictional booking change connects all seven handoffs. The same problem appears at verification and incident recovery. The important failure is an obsolete queued reminder dispatched after the booking update won the defined ordering boundary; an already-submitted message is a separate, explicitly limited promise.

Native radio groups expose fixed views. The challenge uses a small build-time TypeScript policy model to illustrate promotion after canary submission, including current evidence, unknown effects, missing observations, eligible automatic progression and a customer-impact regression. It does not run agents, implement authorization, assess a real system or select a production architecture. No browser JavaScript, framework, runtime service or analytics was added.

The visual emphasis is the selected station's relationship between AI execution, human judgment and its evidence handoff. The numbered rail is a real lifecycle sequence. The order ticket groups an actual acceptance contract, with no decorative Star imagery, chef avatar, fake live metrics or ornamental accent stripe. Existing palette and licensed type remain the visual identity.

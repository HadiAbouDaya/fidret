## Purpose

Describe the requested outcome and how it serves Fidret's purpose. Link relevant company context or decisions.

## Scope

Define deliverables, affected areas, dependencies, and overlap with other tasks. Record who integrates any agreed overlapping work.

## Plan

List the meaningful implementation steps. For a small task, one clear step is sufficient.

## Acceptance criteria

State observable conditions that establish the task is complete.

## Decisions

Link durable decision records. Label unanswered choices and assumptions.

## Verification

Record exact commands or review steps, their results, and any checks not performed.

## Handoff

Record current changes, outstanding questions or blockers, and the next action. Before marking done, record acceptance or the standing authorization used and distinguish implementation completion from publication state.

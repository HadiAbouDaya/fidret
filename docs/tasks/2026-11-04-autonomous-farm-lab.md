## Reader and design intent

The reader follows the relationships between fields, animals, resources, observation and authority. An original farm plan explains the coupled systems; a scenario desk lets the reader decide the next action and inspect the consequences. The simple article links directly to relevant engineering sections. Technical detail belongs on a separate blueprint, keeping the opening readable. Native radios, disclosures and links select authored scenarios; this is not a running digital twin or real telemetry. No runtime/framework or dependency change is planned.

## Acceptance criteria

- Cover open-field vegetables, greenhouse crops, pasture/sheep, dairy cattle/milk, poultry/eggs/meat and their shared water, energy, nutrient, labor, welfare and food-chain dependencies.
- Explain cloud-first sensing/agents/commands with local protective and welfare-critical controls, data quality, command reconciliation, independent authorization and farmer/vet roles.
- Include security, privacy, residency versus sovereignty, remote-access and data lifecycle trade-offs with primary sources and qualified jurisdictional applicability.
- Offer simple explanations with deep links, an advanced blueprint, realistic decision exercises, useful feedback and a portable brief. Label modeled observations and limitations; avoid universal agronomic thresholds and operating prescriptions.
- Review repeatedly for omissions and failure interactions. Test actual policy boundaries and publication withdrawal, not just prose presence.
- Preserve no-JS/CSP, native keyboard controls, visible focus, readable print, reduced motion and narrow/enlarged layouts. All required checks pass.
- Report push, PR, merge and verified live deployment separately.

## Decisions

Retain the accepted static architecture. The cloud-first farm design is a fictional teaching proposal, not an adopted company operating policy. Native controls select authored views; no runtime simulation or equipment connection is introduced.

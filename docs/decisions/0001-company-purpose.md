# Founding purpose and first practice

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-24
- Human decision owner: Hadi Abou Daya
- Basis: Hadi's founding instructions in the initial repository setup conversation

## Context

Fidret needs an enduring purpose and a first area in which to develop expertise. Hadi asked for a foundation that guides the company and work by both Hadi and Elie Azoury through their coding harnesses.

## Decision

Adopt the meaning **Trust earned**, inspired by *fīdere*, and the idea of expertise clients can rely on. Establish physical AI as the first practice. Use the [company charter](../foundation/charter.md) as the canonical expression of this direction.

## Consequences

Company direction stays broader than a particular technology. Work needs a clear purpose, evidence proportionate to its claims, and reliable delivery. First-practice details remain open until they are validated. The collaboration structure must make work understandable to both contributors.

## Review

Revisit through a new decision if the founder changes the purpose or the initial practice direction. Elie's participation does not imply acceptance of decisions he has not reviewed.
